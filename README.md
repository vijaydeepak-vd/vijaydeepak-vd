### 🚀 About Me

I am a Full-Stack JS Developer balancing a 9-to-5 with a deep, focused exploration into the rapidly evolving world of Artificial Intelligence. At my core, I am a massive JavaScript and TypeScript lover who enjoys bridging the gap between modern web architecture, cross-platform development, and applied AI.

Rather than shipping endless side projects, I am currently dedicated to pure learning, researching advanced AI concepts, and documenting my findings through high-quality technical writing.

*   **✍️ Writing:** Breaking down complex AI concepts into practical, developer-first insights on Medium and my Beehiiv newsletter.
